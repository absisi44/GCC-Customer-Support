# 🏬 Gulf E-Commerce Customer Service Policies (GCC)

## 🚚 1. Shipping & Delivery Policy

### 📦 1.1 Domestic Shipping (Saudi Arabia & UAE)
- **Standard Shipping (الشحن العادي)**:
  - **Delivery Time**: 3 to 5 business days across all major cities (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Abu Dhabi).
  - **Shipping Fees**: 25 SAR / 25 AED for orders below 200 SAR / AED.
  - **Free Shipping**: Orders of 200 SAR / 200 AED and above qualify for free standard shipping.
- **Express Shipping (الشحن السريع)**:
  - **Delivery Time**: 1 to 2 business days (available in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dubai).
  - **Shipping Fees**: 45 SAR / 45 AED fixed rate. Not eligible for free shipping waivers.

### 🌍 1.2 Regional GCC Shipping (Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman)
- **Delivery Time**: 4 to 7 business days via regional couriers (Aramex, DHL).
- **Shipping Fees**: Flat fee of 50 SAR / AED (or equivalent local currency).
- **Customs & Duties**: Handled and included in final checkout total; no additional payment upon delivery.

---

## 🔄 2. Returns & Exchange Policy

### ⏳ 2.1 Return Window
- Customers have **14 calendar days** from the date of package delivery to request a return or exchange.

### 📋 2.2 Eligible Conditions
- Products must be in their original packaging, unused, unwashed, and with all original tags, manuals, and accessories attached.
- **Non-returnable items**:
  - Opened cosmetics, fragrances, and personal hygiene products.
  - Underwear and swimwear.
  - Customized or personalized items.
  - Digital gift cards and software licenses.

### 🚚 2.3 Return Process
1. Submit a return request through the mobile app or website under "My Orders" (طلباتي).
2. A prepaid return shipping label (بوليصة إرجاع) will be issued.
3. Courier partner will pick up the shipment from customer's address within 48 to 72 hours.
4. Once the package is received and inspected at our fulfillment center, refund processing begins.

---

## 💰 3. Refunds & Payment Methods

### 💳 3.1 Accepted Payment Methods
- **Mada (مدى)**
- **Apple Pay**
- **Credit / Debit Cards** (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
- **Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)**: **Tamara (تمارا)** and **Tabby (تابي)**
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Available in KSA & UAE with an additional service fee of 15 SAR / AED.

### ⏱️ 3.2 Refund Timelines
- **Credit / Debit Cards & Mada**: 5 to 7 business days depending on customer's issuing bank.
- **Apple Pay**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Store Credit (رصيد المحفظة)**: Instant upon return inspection approval.

### 🛍️ 3.3 Buy Now Pay Later (Tabby & Tamara) Refunds
- When a return is processed for an order paid via **Tamara** or **Tabby**:
  1. Any **remaining unpaid installments are immediately cancelled**.
  2. Any installments already deducted will be **refunded back to the customer's linked bank card** by Tamara/Tabby within 3 to 7 business days.
  3. The customer does not need to continue paying for returned items.

---

## 🚫 4. Order Cancellation & Modification Policy

### ⏱️ 4.1 Cancellation Window
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge within **1 hour of placement**, or as long as the status remains **"Processing" (قيد التجهيز)**.
- Once the order status changes to **"Shipped" (تم الشحن)** or **"Out for Delivery" (خرج للتوصيل)**, the order cannot be cancelled directly and must be processed as a standard return upon receipt.

### 📍 4.2 Changing Shipping Address
- Customers can modify their delivery address before dispatch via customer support or the orders dashboard.

---

## 🛡️ 5. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

- If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect:
  - Customer must report it within **48 hours** of delivery with supporting photos.
  - We provide **100% free immediate replacement or full refund**, including all shipping fees.
